League Rules

  • Entry fee is $135 to be paid prior to the auction
  • Budget is $290
  • Max 10 keepers per year per team at cost
  • Keeper increase structure (+$2, +$3, etc.) increases by number of years kept
  • Undrafted players from previous year cost $5
  • Each player addition costs $1. Trades are free.
  • Transaction fees continue to accrue throughout the playoffs until the conclusion of the final championship game.
  • No player additions are permitted once games begin on the final Sunday of the championship.
  • Category winners are determined based on regular season results only (categories can be split in case of a tie).
  • The amount paid to each category winner is increased by the total transaction fees collected during the playoffs.
  • No trading for future players, compensation, etc.
  • New rules have a 1 season "waiting period" before being implemented
  • Tiebreaker category alternates each year between a hitting ratio stat and a pitching ratio stat
  • Consolation tourney winner receives $160
  • In-season trade deadline date determined each year (same as MLB official trade deadline)
  • Ok to bench as many players as you'd like
  • Must have 8 starting pitchers
  • No trades during off-season (until Winter Meetings start)
  • No adding players from free agency during off-season (even during Winter Meetings)
  • Auction must be in person if at all possible
  • If you are unable to attend the auction you are responsible for sending a proxy
  • Post-season tiebreaker is H2H wins, then total categories
  • Minimum number of innings pitched is 30 to qualify for BAA, ERA, and WHIP
  • Maximum number of pitcher starts is 17 (games started)
  • Playoff teams: 8
  • Weeks per playoff matchup: 1
  • Roster size: 26 (5 bench and 4 IL)
The 2026 column is this year's roster cost; 2027 is what each player would cost to keep next year. Rosters reflect who's on each team right now.
